Integration changed on 2026-10-20: all ten retained Kerr programs now belong to
the independent goden-shader-render.exe. The foobar component contains none of
their bytecode, shader calls, intermediate textures or camera state. It sends
ordinary value inputs and receives only a final image over a public native
protocol. The renderer also runs fixtures from its own command line without
foobar2000, its SDK or the GODEN component. Its GPL-covered combined program is
offered under GPLv3 as a whole, with corresponding source/build/install recipes.
